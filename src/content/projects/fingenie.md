A comprehensive financial management solution featuring AI-powered receipt scanning and innovative social scoring for financial health tracking.

## Unique Features

### Social Score System
- Individual financial health analysis
- Telegram bot integration for instant insights
- Community-based financial wellness tracking

### Smart Expense Management
- AI-powered receipt scanning using ML Kit
- Gemini AI for intelligent expense categorization
- Group expense splitting and management

### Technical Excellence
- Clean architecture principles with BLoC pattern
- Multi-currency support with live conversion rates
- Local-first storage with Hive database

## Backend Infrastructure

Robust backend built with Go and Fiber, powered by PostgreSQL for reliable data management and Python for AI processing.
