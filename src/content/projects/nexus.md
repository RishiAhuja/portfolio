A revolutionary social blogging platform that reimagines content amplification through a unique "signal network" and beacon system.

## Core Concept

Nexus introduces a novel approach to content distribution where posts act as "signals" that can be amplified through a network of "beacons" - creating organic, community-driven content discovery.

## Platform Features

### Signal Amplification
- Unique content distribution algorithm
- Community-driven discovery mechanism
- Beacon system for content curation

### Modern Architecture
- Clean architecture with BLoC pattern
- Responsive Flutter Web implementation
- Firebase backend integration
- Dark/light mode theming

## User Experience

Built for seamless cross-platform experience, Nexus delivers a smooth, intuitive interface that works perfectly across all devices while maintaining high performance.
