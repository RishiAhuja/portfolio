OpenLearn is a comprehensive backend system engineered for cohort-driven educational platforms. Built with TypeScript and Express, it enables institutions to manage structured curricula, track learner progress, assign roles, and foster engagement through badges, leaderboards, and analytics.

## Architecture Overview

With advanced role-based access control (RBAC), OpenLearn supports a multi-tier hierarchy including students (Pioneers), educators (Pathfinders), and admins (Chief Pathfinders, Grand Pathfinders). Courses are delivered through specializations, leagues, and weekly sections—each with multi-format resources and assignments.

## Gamification & Analytics

Gamification and social features are tightly integrated, offering badge systems, progress milestones, and social sharing. The platform includes deep analytics, audit logging, and health monitoring—ensuring visibility and reliability in real-time.

## Production Infrastructure

OpenLearn is built for performance and scalability with Dockerized deployment, PostgreSQL, Redis caching, background email workers, and a fully automated CI/CD pipeline.
