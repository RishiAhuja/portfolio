A comprehensive agricultural cooperative platform built to empower farmers with real-time market insights and AI-powered assistance. Developed under the Annam AI initiative in collaboration with IIT Ropar.

## Key Features

- **Smart Market Tracking**: Real-time agricultural market price monitoring with customizable watchlists
- **AI Farming Assistant**: Intelligent chatbot powered by OpenAI with voice recognition for farming advice
- **Cooperative Management**: Resource sharing and coordination system for farmer cooperatives
- **Offline-First Design**: Robust caching system ensuring functionality without internet connectivity

## Technical Highlights

Built with Flutter and GetX for state management, KisaanMithraa leverages Firebase for backend services and integrates OpenAI for intelligent farming recommendations. The app features a sophisticated offline-first architecture with comprehensive caching, ensuring farmers can access critical information even in areas with poor connectivity.
