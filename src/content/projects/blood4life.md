A comprehensive blood donation platform connecting donors, NGOs, and hospitals in a seamless three-way infrastructure.

## Mission

Blood 4 Life aims to bridge the critical gap between blood donors and those in need through intelligent matching and geographical zone management.

## Key Features

- **Smart Matching**: Automated donor-NGO connection system
- **Zone Management**: Geographical organization for efficient resource allocation
- **Admin Control**: Complete dashboard for managing donations and requests
